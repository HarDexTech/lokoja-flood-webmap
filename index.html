Flood Extent & Risk

Lokoja, Kogi State, Nigeria — 2022 flood event

Overview

Mapped the 2022 flood extent and classified flood-risk zones across the Lokoja study area by combining Sentinel-1 SAR change detection with DEM-based hydrology, then assessed how many people fall inside the flood zone.

Method

  • Sentinel-1 SAR change detection (pre/post flood)
  • DEM hydrology: flow direction, accumulation, stream network, watershed
  • Slope, elevation and flow reclassified into flood-risk zones
  • Population exposure via WorldPop overlay
  • Accuracy assessed against reference points
